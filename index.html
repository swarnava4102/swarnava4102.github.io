About

01

I am a PhD researcher in Decision Sciences and Quantitative Methods at Shailesh J. Mehta School of Management, IIT Bombay. My work sits at a productive tension between theoretical rigor and operational impact — designing intelligent systems that don't just optimize for efficiency, but do so in ways that are fair, interpretable, and deployable.

My primary research vehicle is reinforcement learning, applied to dynamic and combinatorial problems: routing, dispatch, scheduling. I'm particularly interested in how we encode fairness constraints — drawn from healthcare ethics and mechanism design — into systems that govern gig-economy workers and logistics networks.

Outside research, I'm drawn to the translation problem: taking quantitative insight and making it legible to decision-makers who don't speak the language of algorithms. This interest has increasingly pulled me toward management consulting as a career direction.

"The goal is not just to find the optimal policy — it's to find the optimal policy that a fair system would be proud of."
